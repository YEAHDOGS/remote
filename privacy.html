Privacy

EFFECTIVE SEPTEMBER 2026

The app

DOGS Remote collects nothing. Profiles, venue nicknames, and the attempt log live in on-device storage on your phone. Nothing is transmitted, synced, or sold, because there is no server to send it to. The app asks for no runtime permissions and carries no analytics, ads, or trackers.

This site

This landing page sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and stores nothing about you. Downloads come directly from GitHub's release hosting.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/YEAHDOGS/remote/issues.
